## 2. The stitch

One reader in `internal/db` (`rollup_read.go`), used by the
dashboard package and by `logs.go`:

- `RollupWindow(from, to)` splits a window into `head = [from,
  ceil_hour(from))`, `closed = [ceil_hour(from), floor_hour(now))`
  and `live = [floor_hour(now), to]`; `closed` is empty when the
  window sits inside two hours, and the caller then takes the row
  path it takes today (so 15 m and 1 h never change behaviour).
- `ReadHourly(ctx, closed, source, hostID)` returns the
  `log_hourly` rows of the closed span in one statement
  (`hour >= ? AND hour < ?` on `idx_log_hourly_source_hour`, with
  `host_id` when set); `ReadHourlyPaths` the same on
  `log_hourly_paths` (`idx_log_hourly_paths_hour`), summed by path
  in Go, top 10 / 20 as each consumer asks. On prod that is at most
  720 hours x 54 groups for 30 d, 39k rows, a few ms.
- The edges use the statements that exist today, restricted to
  their span: `trafficRows(head)`, `trafficRows(live)`, the
  `ComputeStats` row queries, `computeTimeseriesLong` with
  `from`/`to` set to the edge. Two hours of prod rows is under 4k
  rows.
- Merge rules: counts, bytes and duration sums add; `dur_max_ms`
  is the max; per-hour p50 / p95 / p99 across groups come from the
  summed 8-bucket histogram (the value is the upper edge of the
  bucket that crosses the percentile: 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000,
  2,500, 5,000 ms, or 5,000 for the last bucket), and the live
  hour's exact percentiles from the rows are merged the same way
  by bucketing them first, so one method produces the whole
  series. `avg_duration_ms` is `dur_sum_ms / requests`. The 30 d
  6 h buckets sum six hourly rows before the histogram step.
- Precision statement for the docs: every count, byte and sum over
  a window that starts on an hour boundary is exact (the drift
  checks prove the closed hours; the edges are the rows
  themselves); the long-range percentiles are histogram
  percentiles with the bucket edges above, which is the field's
  documented meaning from this release (today they are exact on a
  sample of 20,000 rows for `logs/stats`, and exact per 15 min or
  hour bucket on rows for the dashboard).
- The 15 min status series of the 24 h traffic view stays exact
  and on rows, but index-only: `SELECT timestamp FROM log_entries
  INDEXED BY idx_log_entries_status_ts WHERE status BETWEEN ? AND ?
  AND timestamp BETWEEN ? AND ?` per class, bucketed in Go; the
  plan is pinned in `TestLongRangePlans` next to the 7 d shape. It
  counts every source with a 2xx-5xx status, as the 7 d bridge does
  today (`waf_audit`, 89 rows per day on prod, 0.2 %); the rollup
  path filters `source = 'caddy_access'` exactly. Documented; the
  alternative (a `(source, status, timestamp)` index) is a schema
  change and is not worth 0.2 %.

Which requests take the rollup path is decided in one place per
endpoint (`statsFastPathEligible` grows the "closed span not empty"
condition and loses the 24 h threshold; `Traffic` dispatches on
`range`), and the response says so: a `X-Argos-Path: rollup |
rows` header on the three endpoints, next to `X-Argos-Cache`, so
the smoke and the operator can tell which path answered without
reading the code.
