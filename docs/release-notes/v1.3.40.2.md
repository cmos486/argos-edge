# v1.3.40.2 -- Raw strip by cursor (hotfix)

Second hotfix on v1.3.40.0. `argosVersion` `1.3.40.1` -> `1.3.40.2`.

## What happened on the v1.3.40.1 deploy

The first purge (+120 s) started emptying `raw` on the ~400k access
rows older than 24 h. Each batch ran
`... WHERE source = ? AND timestamp >= watermark AND timestamp < cutoff AND raw <> '' ORDER BY timestamp LIMIT 5000`;
the watermark only moved at the end of the run, so batch k scanned
the k-1 batches already emptied before finding its rows: quadratic
row visits of 1.4 KB rows, on the single connection, with a 100 ms
pause between batches. Measured on prod while it ran (10:47-10:56
UTC, `/api/hosts` sampled every 100 ms): p50 2 ms, p90 13.2 s,
**p99 51.2 s, max 59.4 s**; 330,000 rows emptied (2026-09-19 to
2026-09-24 08:40) in 10 minutes; WAL 2.1 MB -> 16.3 MB, no
checkpoint until the connection was free.

Mitigation applied at 10:56 UTC without waiting: `PUT
/api/settings/logs.retention.raw_hours = 720` (the cutoff moves past
the oldest row, nothing to strip) and `docker restart
argos-prod-panel`; `/api/hosts` back to 1-3 ms in 15 s. The
watermark was not persisted (v1.3.40.1 wrote it at the end), so the
330k already-empty rows are re-visited once by the new cursor (a
covering index walk plus no-op updates skipped by `raw <> ''`).

## What ships

- `db.stripRawByCursor`: one covering `SELECT id, timestamp` per
  batch of `RawStripBatch` (1,000) ids ordered by `(timestamp, id)`
  from the cursor, one `UPDATE ... WHERE raw <> '' AND id IN (...)`,
  the watermark handed to `PurgePolicy.OnRawProgress` after every
  batch; the retention loop persists it there, so a restart resumes
  at the last completed batch. A cancelled context returns the
  progress made.
- `logs.retention.raw_hours` restored to 24 on prod as part of this
  deploy (the 720 was the mitigation, not a decision).

## Strike 13 and the dense demo seed

The gate that should have caught this (`/api/hosts` p99 during a
purge) ran without prod density: 64 rows on the demo, nothing to
strip on prod. Strike 13 of the pattern, "EFFECT gate without prod
density", distinct from strike 12 (invented schema). The rule in
CLAUDE.md: no release with a new long write deploys before running
it against the dense demo seed (500k `log_entries`, 1.4 KB `raw`,
prod's per-source and per-day distribution), which `scripts/demo`
does not generate yet: known issue with priority in 40.x before the
40.1 rollup backfill.
