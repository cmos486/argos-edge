# v1.3.42.0 -- Hourly rollup: migration 034, fill job, drift check

`argosVersion` `1.3.41.1` -> `1.3.42.0`. First schema change since
v1.3.33. This release only builds and verifies the rollup; **no
reader changes**: the UI keeps reading raw rows. v1.3.42.1 moves the
long ranges onto it once prod has 48 h of drift 0.

## What ships

- **Migration 034** (`034_log_hourly.up.sql` / `.down.sql`): tables
  `log_hourly` (per closed UTC hour, source, host and status class:
  requests, bytes out, duration sum / max, exact p50 / p95 / p99, an
  8-bucket duration histogram, `forbidden` (403), `rate_limited`
  (429) and `errors` (level = error) counters) and `log_hourly_paths`
  (the 50 busiest paths per host per hour), with
  `idx_log_hourly_source_hour` and `idx_log_hourly_paths_hour`.
  Nothing on `log_entries` changes; the four load-bearing indexes
  referenced with `INDEXED BY` are untouched. The down migration
  drops both tables; `TestRollbackLastMigration` peels 034 first.
- **Fill job** (`internal/logs/rollup.go`, `db.FillRollupHour`): runs
  inside the retention goroutine, so it never overlaps the purge. The
  boot backfill follows the boot purge (2 min after start), the
  hourly fill runs at HH:02, the 6 h tick does purge, rollup
  retention and the drift check in that order. Each hour is one
  transaction (clear the hour, grouped pass over its rows through
  `idx_log_entries_timestamp`, exact percentiles, top paths) with a
  100 ms pause between hours. Recovery: every run starts at
  `MAX(hour)` (re-filling the newest hour, so rows that landed after
  its first fill are counted) or at the oldest `log_entries` hour
  when the table is empty, and stops before the hour in progress; a
  lost tick is repaired by the next one or by the next boot. The log
  line `rollup: fill done` carries hours, groups, paths, total time
  and the slowest hour; hours over 3 s are logged one by one.
- **Drift check**: the 6 h tick compares the newest closed hour at
  least 3 h old with the raw rows (`COUNT(*)`, `SUM(size_bytes)`,
  per-class counts; tolerance 0) and persists the verdict
  (`logs.rollup.drift`, `0` or the numbers) and its time; a
  divergence logs `rollup drift` with both sides.
- **Retention**: `logs.rollup_days` (default 90; editable in Settings
  and `PUT /api/settings/logs.rollup_days`), purged by the 6 h tick.
- `GET /api/logs/pipeline` gains `rollup` (`days`, `last_hour`,
  `last_run_at`, `drift`, `drift_checked_at`); Settings shows it
  read-only next to the new days field.
- `scripts/smoke/rollup-agree.sh`: job lag <= 2 h, raw vs rollup for
  a closed hour at tolerance 0 (rows, bytes, per-class), ticker
  verdict.

## Deploy procedure (read before deploying)

1. `POST /api/backups` (VACUUM INTO) and note the filename; wait for
   it to appear in `GET /api/backups`.
2. `make sync-prod-dry`, then `make deploy-prod`; the 42.0 boot
   applies migration 034 (two tables, two indexes, sub-second), and
   2 min later runs the boot purge followed by the backfill (1 to
   3 min on prod behind the 100 ms pause; reads stay on the pool).
3. Rollback logic: none needed for readers (unchanged). If the panel
   is not healthy in 60 s: image rollback below.

### Way back to v1.3.41.1

- Repin `argos-prod-argos:1.3.41.1` in `~/argos-prod/docker-compose.override.yml`
  and `make deploy-prod`. v1.3.41.1 ignores `log_hourly` and
  `log_hourly_paths`; the DB needs nothing.
- Optional cleanup after the repin (never before, the 42.0 boot would
  recreate them): `docker compose exec argos /argos migrate rollback`
  from `~/argos-prod`, which runs `034_log_hourly.down.sql`.
- Last resort: `docker compose exec argos /argos restore --file /data/backups/<name>.tar.gz --yes`
  and `docker compose restart argos` from `~/argos-prod` (the flag
  is applied at the next boot before the DB opens).
