# v1.3.42.2: the community-blocklist pull no longer floods the event queue (2026-09-28)

Patch release between the rollup (42.0), the FilterBar (42.1) and
the rollup readers, which take the **v1.3.42.3** slot from here on
(numbering decided by the operator on 2026-09-28; both planning
docs say so). One backend file, one test, one smoke, no schema, no
bouncer change.

## What was wrong

The 48 h reading of 2026-09-28 (v1.3.40 planning doc, section 11)
found 219,607 `WARN notifications: event queue full, dropping`
lines in the panel log in 40 h, `dropped_total` 230,813, type
`threat_ip_banned`, in 20 bursts of 10-12k every 2 h. The bursts
are the CrowdSec community-blocklist pull: it rewrites its ~15,000
decisions with new IDs, the monitor (`internal/crowdsec/monitor.go`)
diffs decision IDs against the previous 15 s poll and emitted one
`threat_ip_banned` event per new ID, so every pull was 15,000 "new
bans" into the 1,000-slot event queue. Pre-existing since the
monitor was written; the v1.3.42.1 restart put it at the top of a
fresh log. No notification was lost (the `notification_rules` table
is empty on prod); the cost was 21 MB of journal per day and the
CPU of building and dropping 15k events every 2 h.

## What ships

- `notifyPerDecision(origin)`: decisions with origin `CAPI` or
  `lists` (case-insensitive) no longer get a per-decision
  `threat_ip_banned` event. Local origins (`crowdsec`, `cscli`,
  `manual`, the panel's own `argos-country-*`) still notify one by
  one. The `threat_intel_updated` summary is unchanged and keeps the
  added / removed / total counts of every pull.
- `TestMonitorSkipsBlocklistPerDecisionEvents`: a simulated pull of
  15,000 CAPI decisions plus 10 `lists` decisions with new IDs and
  one new local decision produces exactly one `threat_ip_banned`
  (the local one), one summary with `added_count` 15,011, and zero
  drops from the queue. `TestNotifyPerDecision` pins the origins.
- `scripts/smoke/event-storm.sh`, EFFECT: over the last 4 h (two
  pulls at the 2 h cadence, asserted from the crowdsec log) the
  panel log carries zero `event queue full` lines; the panel's log
  volume in MB per day is reported next to the before figure.
- `docs/features/notifications.md`: the two event rows say what
  fires them now.

Host change, outside the repo (operator's decision, 2026-09-28,
reversible): `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/50-argos-maxuse.conf`
with `SystemMaxUse=500M` and `systemctl restart systemd-journald`
on the LXC. Journal 2.7 GB -> 491 MB, disk 87 % -> 78 % (3.6 GB ->
5.9 GB free); the containers kept logging through the restart
(crowdsec 61 lines in the next minute).

`1.3.42.1` and `make deploy-prod`. The restart resets the 6 h drift
check cadence (the ticker starts at the boot purge, +2 min), so the
next two verdicts land at deploy + 2 min + 6 h and + 12 h; the
42.3 gate wants verdicts 7 and 8 at 0, which is why the deploy time
matters (after 11:56 UTC keeps verdict 7 on the old cadence).

After-smokes: `event-storm.sh` at deploy + 4 h with
`BEFORE_MB_PER_DAY=21`, `panel-boot.sh`, `read-pool.sh` gates 0-2,
the boot log (`rollup: fill done` for the hours the restart

## Known issues carried

- AppSec fails open under a scanner burst even at 1 CPU: 16
  `context deadline exceeded` in the two minutes 08:10-08:11 UTC on
  2026-09-28 while one external IP sent 353 requests to one host
  (v1.3.40 planning doc, 6.1). Data point for the bouncer stream
  pre-flight (v1.3.42.4): in stream mode the LAPI stops answering
  one lookup per proxied request (about 59,000 a day) and that CPU
  is AppSec's.
- The monthly `VACUUM` (`maybeVacuum`: day 1, hour 04 UTC) is
  checked at boot and then on a 24 h ticker from the boot time, so
  it only fires when a tick lands inside hour 04 UTC, that is, when
  the panel (re)started between 04:00 and 04:59 UTC. With the
  current boot times it does not run on 2026-10-01. The passive
  sampler for that run is prepared in `scripts/ops/vacuum-sample.sh`
  (not installed); the schedule itself is the operator's decision.
- From v1.3.42.1: the v1.3.41.1 export gate tail (until v1.3.42.3),
  `Until` shown as "expired" on imported decisions,
  `blocked_requests_24h` mixing, `dashboard/cache_test` timing
  flake. The 48 h DB-size reading is closed (v1.3.40 planning doc,
  section 10).
