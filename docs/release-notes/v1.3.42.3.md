# v1.3.42.3: the long-range readers move to the hourly rollup (unreleased)

Planning: `docs/planning/v1.3.42.3-rollup-readers-phase0.md`
(operator's conditions of 2026-09-28: histogram percentiles say so
on the card and api.md lists the edges; deploy only after drift
verdicts 7 and 8 at 0). No schema, no long write, no bouncer change.

## What ships

- One reader in `internal/db/rollup_read.go`: `RollupSpanFor` splits
  a window into the head edge, the closed hours the rollup holds and
  the live tail; `ReadRollupHours` / `ReadRollupPaths` read the
  closed span through the rollup indexes; `Hist` merges the 8-bucket
  duration histogram of rollup rows and live rows and resolves
  nearest-rank percentiles to the bucket edge (the merged maximum
  above 5,000 ms).
- Dashboard traffic: 1h and 6h unchanged on rows (`percentile_method:
  exact`); 24h, 7d and the new 30d stitched (`histogram`): the 24 h
  status series stays at 15 min from an index-only scan of the status
  index, response times one point per hour (6 h on 30d), top hosts,
  top paths and bandwidth over the whole range. Overview counters the
  same way. The pinned 24 h refresh no longer visits the rows that
  carry `raw`.
- `logs/stats` and `logs/timeseries` (hourly buckets) on time-only
  filters with at least one closed hour in the window: stitched; the
  covering-index path of v1.3.38.4 stays only as the fallback while
  the rollup has no hours (`percentile_method: sample`).
- `X-Argos-Path: rollup | rows` on overview, traffic, stats and
  timeseries; `percentile_method` in the traffic and stats bodies.
  Gone: `detail_window`, `detail_from`, `series_covers_range`,
  `sample_n`, the "(last N h of range)" card titles.
- Endpoints take the URL vocabulary: `range` on `/api/logs`,
  `/api/logs/stats`, `/api/logs/timeseries`, `/api/logs/export.csv`,
  `/api/notifications/deliveries` (alternative to `from`/`to`) and
  `/api/appsec/metrics` (alias of `window`); `q` on
  `/api/threats/decisions`. `lib/filters.ts` lost `rangeFrom`,
  `rangeToDash` and `rangeToAppSecWindow`; the pages send `range` as
  is. Dashboard presets 1h / 6h / 24h / 7d / 30d (the security
  section clamps 30d to 7d, it stays on rows); Logs presets gain 30d;
  the p95 cards say "histogram (bucket edges)" with the edges in the
  tooltip.

- Monthly VACUUM on the calendar (1st of the month, 04:00 UTC) with a
  catch-up at boot when the recorded run is older than the last slot;
  the first boot with this code only records the last slot, so on
  prod the first run is 2026-10-01 04:00 UTC (the sampler
  `scripts/ops/vacuum-sample.sh` is for that run). The old check ran
  on a 24 h ticker from boot and never fired unless the panel had
  started inside hour 04 UTC.
- `read-pool.sh`: `STRIP_WAIT_S` (120 s) and an explicit PASS
  "nothing to strip" instead of waiting `CAP_S` for a purge line the
  panel only writes when it had work.

Found and fixed during the run: with an empty rollup (the minutes
before the first fill, or a reset) 30d fell back to the row path and
hit the loader deadline on the dense seed; long ranges without a
rollup hour now answer the series and top hosts from the indexes
with a `note` (commit `2c134cd`). The demo also showed that
`read-pool.sh` waits forever for `retention purge done` when the
strip has nothing to do (the panel logs the line only when it
removed or stripped rows); with `raw_hours` 720 on the demo the
default `STRIP_HOURS=6` strips nothing, hence `STRIP_HOURS=600`.

## Known issues carried

From v1.3.42.2: AppSec fail-open under a scanner burst at 1 CPU
(stream pre-flight data point), `Until` on imported decisions,
`blocked_requests_24h` mixing, `dashboard/cache_test` timing flake.
The VACUUM schedule bound to the boot hour is fixed here. New: traffic
24h with a host filter still reads that host's rows (2.6 s cold for
the busiest host on the dense demo; on demand, not pinned); hourly
from the rollup in v1.3.42.3.1 or 43. On the rollup path `top_hosts`
counts rows with a host link and `top_paths` counts access rows
(api.md).
