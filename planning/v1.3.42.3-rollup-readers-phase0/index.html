    v1.3.42.3 PHASE 0: the readers move to the hourly rollup (2026-09-28)¶

    Numbering note (operator, 2026-09-28): the threat_ip_banned event storm found in the 48 h window ships first as v1.3.42.2 (one file in crowdsec/monitor.go, its test and event-storm.sh); the readers described here are v1.3.42.3. Every earlier reference to "v1.3.42.2" for the readers (42.1 release note, api.md until this commit) means this release.

    Read-only pass over the code as it is after v1.3.42.1, against section 9 of v1.3.40-log-pipeline-phase0.md (what the rollup serves, 9.1) and the 42.1 notes that deferred three API alignments to this release. Scope, as recorded on 2026-09-26: the long-range readers (dashboard traffic 7 d and the new 30 d, logs/stats, logs/timeseries, the overview counters and the pinned 24 h refresh) read log_hourly / log_hourly_paths and stitch the edges from log_entries; the v1.3.38.4 bridge fields (detail_window, detail_from, series_covers_range, sample_n) and the "last 24 h of range" card labels go; api.md and the endpoints take the URL vocabulary (q on Threats, range everywhere) so lib/filters.ts loses its translation; gate 3 of v1.3.41.1 (export x3 with /api/hosts p99 <= 50 ms) is repeated once the pinned refresh no longer visits raw rows. No schema change: migration 034 already carries every column this needs (forbidden, rate_limited, errors, the histogram).

    2. The stitch¶

    One reader in internal/db (rollup_read.go), used by the dashboard package and by logs.go:

    • RollupWindow(from, to) splits a window into head = [from, ceil_hour(from)), closed = [ceil_hour(from), floor_hour(now)) and live = [floor_hour(now), to]; closed is empty when the window sits inside two hours, and the caller then takes the row path it takes today (so 15 m and 1 h never change behaviour).
    • ReadHourly(ctx, closed, source, hostID) returns the log_hourly rows of the closed span in one statement (hour >= ? AND hour < ? on idx_log_hourly_source_hour, with host_id when set); ReadHourlyPaths the same on log_hourly_paths (idx_log_hourly_paths_hour), summed by path in Go, top 10 / 20 as each consumer asks. On prod that is at most 720 hours x 54 groups for 30 d, 39k rows, a few ms.
    • The edges use the statements that exist today, restricted to their span: trafficRows(head), trafficRows(live), the ComputeStats row queries, computeTimeseriesLong with from/to set to the edge. Two hours of prod rows is under 4k rows.
    • Merge rules: counts, bytes and duration sums add; dur_max_ms is the max; per-hour p50 / p95 / p99 across groups come from the summed 8-bucket histogram (the value is the upper edge of the bucket that crosses the percentile: 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000 ms, or 5,000 for the last bucket), and the live hour's exact percentiles from the rows are merged the same way by bucketing them first, so one method produces the whole series. avg_duration_ms is dur_sum_ms / requests. The 30 d 6 h buckets sum six hourly rows before the histogram step.
    • Precision statement for the docs: every count, byte and sum over a window that starts on an hour boundary is exact (the drift checks prove the closed hours; the edges are the rows themselves); the long-range percentiles are histogram percentiles with the bucket edges above, which is the field's documented meaning from this release (today they are exact on a sample of 20,000 rows for logs/stats, and exact per 15 min or hour bucket on rows for the dashboard).
    • The 15 min status series of the 24 h traffic view stays exact and on rows, but index-only: SELECT timestamp FROM log_entries INDEXED BY idx_log_entries_status_ts WHERE status BETWEEN ? AND ? AND timestamp BETWEEN ? AND ? per class, bucketed in Go; the plan is pinned in TestLongRangePlans next to the 7 d shape. It counts every source with a 2xx-5xx status, as the 7 d bridge does today (waf_audit, 89 rows per day on prod, 0.2 %); the rollup path filters source = 'caddy_access' exactly. Documented; the alternative (a (source, status, timestamp) index) is a schema change and is not worth 0.2 %.

    Which requests take the rollup path is decided in one place per endpoint (statsFastPathEligible grows the "closed span not empty" condition and loses the 24 h threshold; Traffic dispatches on range), and the response says so: a X-Argos-Path: rollup | rows header on the three endpoints, next to X-Argos-Cache, so the smoke and the operator can tell which path answered without reading the code.
